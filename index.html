ABOUT I-RECORD

Simplifying Research and Extension Records

I-Record is a web-based record management system developed for the Office of the Vice President for Research and Extension.

The system provides a centralized platform for storing, organizing, monitoring, and retrieving digital research and extension records.

It aims to reduce manual paperwork, prevent misplaced records, improve document organization, and make information retrieval faster and more efficient.
